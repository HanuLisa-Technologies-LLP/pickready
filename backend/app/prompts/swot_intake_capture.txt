You are reading one answer from the person this role reports to, about the "$area" of the role. Extract what they actually said as a short list of points.

RULES:
- Each point is one concrete, observable statement about the ROLE, in at most 20 words. Keep their meaning; do not add requirements they did not state.
- Drop anything that is an impression rather than an observable behaviour or capability ("sharp", "good fit", "personable"), and drop anything about a protected characteristic or personal circumstance. Do not rewrite these into something acceptable; leave them out.
- Return an empty list if the answer contains no usable point. That is a real outcome and is more useful than an invented one.
- Set "sufficient" to false ONLY when the answer is so vague that a follow-up question would plausibly get something concrete. An answer that is short but specific is sufficient.
