# v2 (case 2): the EXAMPLES block below. Whether this email has anything to say

# prompt is rendered, so by the time the model reads this, hedging about the

You are writing an INTERNAL reminder to a recruiter at {company_name}. This is
email type 6 of 6, and the only one whose recipient is a colleague rather than
a candidate.

Recruiter Name: {recruiter_name}
Job Title: {job_title}
Company: {company_name}
Hours since the job was created: {hours_elapsed}
Link to the job: {job_link}

Write a brief, businesslike internal note of 70-110 words that:
1. States which job needs attention and how long it has been waiting.
2. Says what is blocked until they look at it  -  candidates cannot be assessed
   against this role.
3. Includes the job link on its own line, exactly as given above.

Rules:
- Address the recruiter by name.
- Colleague-to-colleague tone: direct and short. No marketing voice, no
  candidate-facing warmth, no apologising for the reminder.
- Do not mention scores, rankings, or percentages.
- Plain prose. No markdown, no bullet characters, no emoji.

Return ONLY valid JSON:
{{
  "subject": "Subject line here",
  "body": "Email body here as plain text with blank lines between paragraphs"
}}

Do not include markdown, triple backticks, commentary, or explanations. Return only JSON.

EXAMPLES

GOOD EXAMPLE
Body: "Hi Sunita, the Warehouse Supervisor job has been waiting for your review
since it was created, some time ago now. Until it is reviewed, candidates cannot
be assessed against this role.

https://app.example.test/org/jobs/abc123"
Why it is right: colleague to colleague, states the blockage in one line, and
carries the link exactly as given. No warmth performance, no apology.

BAD EXAMPLE, DO NOT WRITE ANYTHING LIKE THIS
Body: "Hi Sunita, our records do not establish how long this job has been
waiting, and it could not be verified whether the framework was generated.
Please review it when convenient."
Why it is wrong, and this is the whole reason the block exists: it describes the
state of our own records instead of speaking to the person receiving it. Never
do this. Whether there was enough to write from was settled before this prompt
ran, so a hedge here is a false statement about a decision already made, sent to
someone's inbox under the company's name.
END BAD EXAMPLE

EDGE CASE
The hours elapsed you were given is a vague value such as "some time". Say it
plainly in words and move on. This reader is a colleague with a queue of work,
so an extra sentence explaining that the exact figure is unavailable wastes the
one thing the note is meant to save them.
