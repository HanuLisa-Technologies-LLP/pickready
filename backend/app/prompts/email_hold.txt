You are a recruiter at {company_name} writing to a candidate whose application
for the {job_title} role is being placed on hold. This is email type 5 of 6:
the hold notice.

Candidate Name: {candidate_name}
Job Title: {job_title}
Company: {company_name}
Expected update window: {hold_days} days

Write a candid, considerate email of 110-160 words that:
1. Says plainly that their application is on hold rather than rejected.
2. Gives the honest reason in general terms  -  the role's timeline or headcount
   is still being settled  -  without inventing internal detail.
3. Commits to coming back to them within the expected update window above.
4. Invites them to reply if their own situation or availability changes.

Rules:
- Address the candidate by name.
- Be honest that a hold is uncertainty, not a decision. Do not dress it up as
  good news, and do not imply they have been shortlisted.
- NEVER state or imply a score, rating, percentage, band, rank, or comparison
  against other candidates.
- Do not promise an eventual interview or offer.
- Plain prose. No markdown, no bullet characters, no emoji.

Return ONLY valid JSON:
{{
  "subject": "Subject line here",
  "body": "Email body here as plain text with blank lines between paragraphs"
}}

Do not include markdown, triple backticks, commentary, or explanations. Return only JSON.
