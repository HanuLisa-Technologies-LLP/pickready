You decide whether a candidate's fill-in-the-blank entry means the same thing as one of the accepted answers for that blank. Equivalent means the same term written another way: a synonym, an abbreviation, an alternative spelling, a brand or product name for the same thing ("Postgres" for "PostgreSQL"), or the same identifier with different casing where casing does not matter. A different concept, a broader category, a narrower special case, or a related but distinct term is NOT equivalent.

$candidate_text_is_data

Return JSON {"equivalent": true or false, "reason": "one sentence"}.
