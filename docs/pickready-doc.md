PICKREADY
Proprietary Platform Concept
Hanulisa Technologies LLP · Internal Concept Note · July 2026
1. Client Authentication — OTP Login
Client logs in using their official email ID and mobile phone number. An OTP is sent to both — there is no password. This validates both the email and the mobile number of the client.
After this initial validation, the client can log in using either the email or the mobile number, by entering the OTP received on that channel.
If the client wants to change their registered email ID or phone number, the same OTP validation process above applies again.
2. Client & Company Onboarding
After login, the client creates their company page: company brief, culture, policies, benefits.
The client also creates login access for their hiring managers from this page.
3. Job Creation & Multi-Level Approval Workflow
When a new job is raised, the concerned hiring manager creates a detailed JD covering: Job Title, Department, Level, Reporting To, Reportees, Role, Responsibilities, Accountabilities, Education, Skills, Experience (in years), and Requirement period.
Once created, the job is routed through up to four levels of higher authority: Requested → Recommended → Approved → Ratified.
Not all four levels are mandatory — the client decides how many levels apply, and who sits at each one.
4. HR Sourcing, Databank Matching & AI Contextual Rating
Once ratified, the job reaches HR. HR has the authority to add compensation and to modify the JD to resolve ambiguity or add missing detail.
The moment the job reaches HR, Pickready automatically surfaces matching profiles from the existing databank — candidates already validated on earlier jobs who consented under Aspect 40 to be matched against future opportunities — as a starting shortlist for the Recruiter. This is a supporting hand, not a replacement for sourcing.
The Recruiter reviews the databank shortlist and sources additional resumes through their own channels to fill any remaining gaps, holding freshly sourced resumes in a folder.
The freshly sourced resumes are uploaded into Pickready and linked to the corresponding job; databank matches are linked automatically and require no upload.
Pickready's AI then provides a contextual rating for every linked profile — both databank matches and freshly sourced — based on fit against the job description, using four match tiers: Highly Matching (≥90%), Moderately Matching (≥70%), Matching (≥50%), Not Matching (<50%).
Tiers are evaluated top-down (highest threshold first), so a profile scoring exactly at a boundary — e.g. 90% — falls into the higher tier, not both.
5. Candidate Outreach — Data & Employer-Verification Requirements
Applies to freshly sourced candidates only. Databank matches (Section 4) already hold a validated Profile from an earlier job and are not sent through this process again — their existing Profile is reused as-is.
HR emails the candidates selected by the Recruiter from the uploaded bunch, requesting:
Full Name — as per PF records or Class X memorandum
Residing City
Age
Gender
Updated resume
The 40-aspect questionnaire — where any of (a)–(d) above duplicates a 40-aspect question, that aspect is not asked twice
Official HR email IDs of up to three previous employers, so Pickready can send each one an automated verification email requesting: Designation, DOJ, DOE, Last Drawn CTC, Last Drawn Gross, NOC status, completion of exit formalities, whether a BGV was conducted, and details on educational certificates, address proof, ID proof, previous experience, and previous compensation. This verification step does not apply to the candidate's present employer — verifying with a current employer requires a separate, consent-based initiative led by the Recruiter.
All of the above is mandatory before the Recruiter can proceed.
6. Candidate Response, Application & Data Parsing
The candidate receives a link to apply for the job and responds to the above requirements on their own Candidate Page.
The information received from candidates is parsed into the product against each candidate's record.
7. HR Review Screen
On the home screen, once candidate responses are received: candidate names are listed on the left, and — for the selected candidate — all 40 aspects with their responses are displayed on the right.
Terminology: the full detail set captured for a candidate — resume, 40 aspects, and employer-verification responses together — is referred to as the candidate's "Profile" throughout the product.
8. Hiring Manager Shortlisting, Interview Scheduling & Status Tracking
HR reviews the profiles and grants access to Hiring Manager 1 (or any of up to four hiring managers, per the levels configured in Section 3).
The hiring manager(s) shortlist profiles and instruct HR via three platform buttons: Rejected, Shortlisted, Hold — with a mandatory remarks field on Hold.
Based on these button actions, the Recruiter schedules interviews through the platform, using the client's official email ID only — no Gmail or Outlook integration, since clients rely solely on their own official email domain.
During and after the interview process, the Recruiter or HR must update every profile's status: Rejected, Shortlisted, Offered, Joined. This update is mandatory.
No email templates are provided by Pickready — each employer uses its own templates.
9. Candidate Portal
Candidates have a separate login to track their application: New Jobs (visible only to candidates who have already received a first outreach email from an employer on the platform), Apply for Jobs, and Status of Application Stages.
The candidate portal never stores the candidate's resume — a fresh resume must be attached for every job application.
